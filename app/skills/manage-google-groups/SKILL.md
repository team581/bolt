# Manage Google Groups

- Team members use their personal Google accounts registered as external accounts in the group
- Students are added to `students@team581.com` so they can access shared team resources
- Coaches belong to `coaches@team581.com`, which grants more privileged access
  - Saikiran or Jonah handle this manually
